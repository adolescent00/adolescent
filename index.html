08 /Questions, answered

Frequently asked
questions.

Short answers to the things business owners ask us most. Anything else — just ask.

Ask us a question →
Do I need to pay monthly?+

No. You can choose a monthly plan (₹5,000, ₹8,000 or ₹12,000/month) that includes hosting and maintenance, or buy your website once for ₹25,000 with optional maintenance afterwards.

What is included in the monthly plans?+

Design and build of your website, hosting, SSL, backups, security updates, uptime monitoring, basic SEO and a monthly allowance of small updates. Growth and Premium add landing pages, tracking, reporting and more — see the comparison table above.

What happens if I cancel?+

You can cancel as set out in your service agreement. Hosting and maintenance stop at the end of the paid period, and we’ll discuss handover options with you. The exact cancellation, ownership and handover terms are in the agreement you sign, so nothing is left to memory.

Who manages the hosting?+

We do, for as long as your monthly subscription is active. For the one-time website, we set up hosting on an account in your name; the hosting fee is paid to the provider, and we can manage it for you under the optional maintenance plan.

Can I buy the website instead?+

Yes. The one-time website is ₹25,000 for up to 7 pages, including testing, launch and 30 days of bug support. Domain and hosting fees are separate, and maintenance after 30 days is optional at ₹4,000/month.

Can you update my website later?+

Yes. Monthly plans include 2, 5 or 10 small update requests per month depending on the plan. Larger changes, such as new features or many new pages, are quoted separately before any work starts.

Do you provide SEO?+

All plans include basic on-page SEO: sensible page titles, descriptions, structure and Google setup. Growth adds Search Console, analytics and a monthly visibility check; Premium adds keyword research and monthly improvements. We don’t guarantee rankings — nobody honestly can.

Is the domain included?+

We handle the domain setup on every plan, and the domain is registered in your business’s name. The registrar’s annual fee is billed separately at cost, so you always own your address.

How long does a website take to build?+

Most Starter and one-time websites take around 2–3 weeks from receiving your content; larger sites take longer. The biggest factor is how quickly we get your text, photos and feedback — we’ll give you a clear timeline before we begin.
