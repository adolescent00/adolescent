§ 14 — QUESTIONS

BEFORE YOU START.

01What happens during the first session?

A short conversation about your training background and goals, a coached movement warm-up, and a scaled version of the day's session. You leave knowing how the floor works and which track suits you.

02Do I need previous training experience?

No. Every session is scaled by the coach on the floor. Beginners usually start in Coached sessions, where technique is taught before load is added.

03What should I bring?

Training clothes, flat-soled shoes if you have them, and a water bottle. Chalk, bands and everything else is on the floor.

04Which training format should I start with?

Most people start with Coached: it covers strength and conditioning in a structured week. If you already train regularly and want programming and reviews, Performance may fit better. The intro session helps you decide.

05Can I attend group sessions?

Yes, on the Coached and Performance tracks. Groups are kept small so the coach can watch every rep that matters.

06How does the free week work?

In this concept, a free week gives you open-floor access and up to three coached sessions with no commitment afterwards. (Fictional offer for demonstration.)

07Do you offer individual coaching?

Individual programming and monthly reviews are part of the Performance track. One-to-one sessions can be arranged directly with a coach depending on availability.
